Location-aware apps, built properly.

We build interactive iOS apps that turn physical places into experiences people pay for. You supply the destination and the story. We build, ship and maintain the app.

Built for the wild

Consumer travel apps fail because they drop out in remote areas and crash during peak season. Experiential travel needs a different architecture.

We build on cloud-native infrastructure that handles tourist surges, and we run it for you.

  • No IT team required. We manage server scaling, database security and OS updates.
  • Actionable analytics. Verified dwell metrics on where visitors actually spend their time.
  • Maintained, not delivered. The app doesn't stop being our problem the day it ships.

Location-triggered engagement

Stop giving premium content away to people sitting on their couches. We build proximity checks that only unlock digital rewards, hidden audio guides and partner discounts when a user is physically present at your coordinates.

Radius, dwell time and verification strictness are set per project, because a summit cairn and a high street café need different rules.

Zero-signal operation

Real exploration happens off the grid. If your app dies when a customer loses cellular service, you lose the customer.

We cache the entire interactive map and digital inventory locally on the device. Users navigate trails, check into waypoints and unlock badges completely offline. Progress syncs the moment they're back in range.

Shareable completions

Turn customers into your marketing channel. We build branded completion screens, like topographical journey maps and stamped badges, that users share to Instagram and TikTok, driving organic traffic back to you.

Your brand, your App Store account

We build fully branded native apps that ship under your own Apple Developer account, in your company's name. You own the listing, the customer relationship and the revenue. Apple pays you directly.

You add us as an admin and we handle the build, the submission, review responses and every update afterward.

Every app is designed around what you're actually offering. We don't run a template.

Integrating with what you have

Already have a mobile app or corporate portal? You don't need to start over. We can build proximity and gamification into your existing codebase instead: location-based rewards, verified check-ins, and interaction data synced to your existing CRM or loyalty programme.
