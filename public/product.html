Location-Triggered Engagement

Stop giving your premium content away to people sitting on their couches. Our proximity engine ensures that digital rewards, hidden audio guides, and partner discounts are only unlocked when a user is physically present at your designated real-world coordinates.

import { Engine } from '@blacksands/core';

// Define your physical reward zone
const destination = {
    target: "historic_summit",
    requiredRadius: 20 // meters
};

// The Engine verifies their physical presence
if (await Engine.verifyArrival(destination)) {
    unlockExclusiveAudioGuide();
    issueDigitalExplorerBadge();
}

Zero-Signal Operation

Real exploration happens off the grid. If your app dies when a customer loses cellular service, you lose the customer. The Black Sands engine caches your entire interactive map and digital inventory locally on the user's device. They can navigate trails, check into waypoints, and unlock badges completely offline. The moment they return to civilization, the engine silently syncs their progress to the cloud.
