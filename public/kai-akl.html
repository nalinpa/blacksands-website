Privacy Policy

Effective Date:

Black Sands Digital ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Kai AKL mobile application (the "App"). Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

  • Personal Data: When you register an account, we collect identifiable information such as your email address and a unique user identifier created via our authentication provider.
  • Geo-Location Information: We request access or permission to track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously or while you are using the App, to provide location-based services (such as calculating the distance to nearby venues and unlocking location-based badges).
  • Camera & Media Access: We request access to your device's camera to allow you to take photos of your meals and share them. These photos remain entirely on your local device. We do not upload or collect your personal photos on our servers.
  • Mobile Device Data: We automatically collect device information, such as your mobile device ID, model, operating system, and anonymous crash data to improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customised experience. Specifically, we use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account and personal tasting ledger.
  • Verify your proximity to venues for check-ins.
  • Monitor and analyse usage and trends to improve your experience with the App.
  • Secure your data across multiple devices.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We share your information only with third-party service providers that perform services for us or on our behalf. These include:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication and securely storing your completed dishes, reviews, and unlocked badges (Cloud Firestore).
  • Expo: Used for application framework infrastructure, over-the-air updates, and anonymous crash reporting.

4. Your Rights (New Zealand Privacy Act 2020)

In accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and globally recognised privacy standards, you have the right to ask for a copy of any personal information we hold about you, and to ask for it to be corrected if you think it is wrong. You also maintain the right to withdraw your consent for location tracking at any time via your device's system settings.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You can delete your account and permanently erase your tasting history at any time. To do this:

  • Open the Kai AKL App and navigate to the Account screen.
  • Tap on Delete Account & Data and confirm your choice.

Upon confirmation, your email, unique identifier, and checklist history are permanently expunged from our active Firebase databases.
