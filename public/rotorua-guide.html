Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Black Sands Digital ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Rotorua Guide mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier. You may also sign in with Apple or Google, in which case we receive the identifier and email address those services provide.
  • Location Data: Rotorua Guide requires your precise location (GPS) to verify your presence at points of interest for check-ins. This data is processed in real-time on-device and is not stored continuously.
  • Photos: If you choose to create a share card, you may select a photo from your camera or photo library. This photo is used only to generate the image you choose to share and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Reviews: Ratings and text reviews you submit for a location are stored against your account and are visible to other users of the app.
  • Purchase Information: If you unlock the full guide, Apple processes your payment. We receive a purchase token used solely to verify and grant your entitlement.
  • Usage Data & Crash Reports: We collect anonymous diagnostic data to help us identify bugs and improve the app's performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the app's core functionality. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal location history or account details with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Rotorua Guide utilises Google Firebase to securely authenticate users and store your account data, saved sites, and itineraries. Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google are used only for authentication. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. Your data is handled in accordance with each provider's own privacy and security protocols.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your account and associated data at any time directly via the settings menu inside the Rotorua Guide application. Upon requesting deletion, your data is permanently removed from our active database.

5. Children's Privacy

Rotorua Guide does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not attempt to register for the app or send any personal information about yourself to us.
