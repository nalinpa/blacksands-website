1. Telemetry & Location Data

The Black Sands engine utilizes the Haversine formula to verify physical proximity. We only process user coordinates locally on the device at the moment a check-in is requested. Continuous background location tracking is never enabled by default unless explicitly requested and configured by the operating client.

2. Data Ownership (Processor vs. Controller)

Operators retain 100% ownership of their user data. Black Sands acts solely as the data processor, utilizing our cloud architecture to securely route authentication credentials, offline-sync payloads, and digital unlock progress. We claim no ownership over your audience.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell telemetry, waypoint data, or user profiles to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is only shared with the underlying infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud and Firebase) strictly required to maintain system state and deliver the API.
