Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

Black Sands Digital ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Guide AKL mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you create an account to save your progress, we collect your email address and a unique user identifier.
  • Location Data: Guide AKL requires your precise location (GPS) to unlock nearby content layers and verify your presence at points of interest. This data is processed in real-time on-device and is not stored continuously.
  • Usage Data & Crash Reports: We collect anonymous diagnostic data to help us identify bugs and improve the app's performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the app's core functionality. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal location history or account details with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Third-Party Services

Guide AKL utilises Google Firebase to securely authenticate users and store your visited location history. Your data is handled in accordance with Google's privacy and security protocols.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can completely delete your account and your visited history at any time directly via the settings menu inside the Guide AKL application. Upon requesting deletion, your data is permanently removed from our active database.

5. Children's Privacy

Guide AKL does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not attempt to register for the app or send any personal information about yourself to us.
