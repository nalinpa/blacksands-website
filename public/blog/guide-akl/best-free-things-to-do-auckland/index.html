Best Free Things to Do in Auckland

The best free things to do in Auckland are climbing a volcanic cone, walking the waterfront, spending time at the beaches, and exploring the parks and gardens. The city's best moments are often outdoors and cost nothing.

What are the best free things to do in Auckland?

Auckland rewards the outdoors, and most of it is free.

Climb a volcano

The cones are the city's signature free activity. Maungawhau (Mount Eden) gives a 360-degree view over the whole isthmus in a 20-minute walk, and Maungakiekie (One Tree Hill) is a gentle climb through parkland. North Head in Devonport adds historic tunnels to explore. All are free, and the views beat anything you pay for.

Walk the waterfront and the beaches

The harbour edge is made for wandering. The Viaduct and Wynyard Quarter are easy and central, lined with boats and open space. For sand, Mission Bay, Takapuna and Point Chevalier are all free city beaches, good for a swim in summer or a walk any time. The Devonport ferry costs a little but the views across the harbour are worth it.

Explore the parks and gardens

Auckland's green spaces cost nothing. The Auckland Domain, the city's oldest park, sits in a volcanic crater and holds the Wintergardens and the museum grounds. Cornwall Park around One Tree Hill is a working farm park with space to roam. The Parnell Rose Gardens and Western Springs are easy, pretty and free. Together they make a full day without spending.

What about rainy days?

A few free options hold up in the wet. The Auckland Art Gallery has free general entry, the Wintergardens in the Domain are under glass, and wandering the shops and lanes of Britomart and the High Street district keeps you mostly covered. For everything else, Auckland's free pleasures are outdoor ones, so a fine day is when the city is at its cheapest and best.
