Auckland in One Day: What to Prioritise

With one day in Auckland, prioritise climbing a volcano for the view, walking the waterfront, and taking a short ferry to Devonport or an island. Add a good meal off the waterfront and you have the shape of the city in a day.

How should you spend one day in Auckland?

Hit the high point, the harbour and the suburbs. In a single day you can climb a cone for the view, see the waterfront, get out on the water briefly, and eat well, which between them give a real sense of Auckland. The city is compact enough to do this without rushing if you plan a loose order and use the ferries and buses.

Morning: climb a cone

Start high. Maungawhau (Mount Eden) is a short climb to the best 360-degree view in the city, taking in the skyline, both harbours and the other cones. Go early to beat the crowds and the heat. It orients you to the whole city in 20 minutes of walking and is the single best way to start an Auckland day.

Midday: waterfront and a ferry

Come down to the harbour. Walk the Viaduct and Wynyard Quarter, then take the short Devonport ferry across for village charm and a quick climb up Mount Victoria, or ride to Rangitoto if you have longer and want a proper walk. The ferry itself, out on the harbour with the city behind, is part of the day's best.

Afternoon and evening: eat in the suburbs

Finish where the locals eat. Skip the waterfront chains and head to the suburbs, Ponsonby for polished dining, Dominion or Sandringham Road for cheaper and excellent Asian and South Asian food, or K Road for a livelier evening. A good meal off the harbour is the right way to end a day that started on a volcano and ran along the water.
