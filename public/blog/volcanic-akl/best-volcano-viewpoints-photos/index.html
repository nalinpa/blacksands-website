Best Volcano Viewpoints for Auckland City Photos

For Auckland city photos, Maungawhau (Mount Eden) gives the widest skyline shot, Maungakiekie (One Tree Hill) frames the city behind its obelisk, and Mount Victoria in Devonport captures the skyline across the harbour. All are free and quick to reach.

Where are the best volcano viewpoints for city photos?

Three cones stand out for shooting Auckland.

Maungawhau (Mount Eden) for the skyline

This is the classic shot. From the highest point on the isthmus you get a clean 360-degree sweep, with the Sky Tower and CBD on one side and the crater, harbours and Rangitoto on the others. The viewing platform closest to the road gives the best straight-on city and Sky Tower view. Sunrise and sunset are the strongest light, and the crater adds a dramatic foreground.

Maungakiekie (One Tree Hill) for the obelisk

One Tree Hill gives you a landmark in the frame. The tall summit obelisk works as a foreground subject with the city stretching out behind, and the surrounding Cornwall Park parkland and avenues of trees give softer options lower down. It is a different, more composed shot than Mount Eden's raw panorama.

Mount Victoria (Takarunga) for the harbour

For the city across the water, cross to Devonport. Mount Victoria is a short climb above the village and looks back over the Waitematā Harbour to the Auckland skyline, with boats and ferries in the foreground. Pair it with North Head nearby for harbour-mouth angles. The ferry ride over is part of the appeal.

When is the best light?

Sunrise and the golden hour before sunset, as with most cityscapes. Mount Eden faces a broad arc so it works for both, while a harbour view from Devonport catches warm evening light on the city face. Clear days after rain give the sharpest air. Whatever the cone, get there early to claim your spot before the crowds.
