What Is the Best Volcano to Climb in Auckland?

The best volcano to climb in Auckland is Maungawhau (Mount Eden). It is the highest cone on the isthmus, the walk up takes under 20 minutes, and the summit gives a full 360-degree view over the city, both harbours and out to Rangitoto. It is free.

What is the best volcano to climb in Auckland?

Maungawhau (Mount Eden) is the pick for most people. At 196 metres it is the highest natural point on the isthmus, yet the climb is short and the reward is the best all-round view in the city, taking in the skyline, both harbours and the other cones dotted across Auckland. The summit also overlooks a striking grassed crater that is 50 metres deep. If you only climb one, climb this one.

How long does it take to walk up Mount Eden?

Around 10 to 20 minutes from the lower car park, depending on your pace and where you start. The full summit loop is roughly 2 kilometres. Vehicle access to the top was closed in 2016 to protect the maunga, so everyone walks now, on formed paths that are steepish but manageable for most people. There is a boardwalk around the crater rim at the top.

What are the best alternatives?

If you want a different character, the options are good. Maungakiekie (One Tree Hill) is a gentler, longer walk through Cornwall Park with an obelisk at the summit. Rangitoto is a bigger day out, a ferry ride then about an hour's climb through lava fields. North Head and Mount Victoria in Devonport are short climbs with harbour views and, at North Head, historic tunnels to explore.

Is it free and how do you get there?

Yes, Mount Eden is free. It sits a couple of kilometres south of the city centre, reachable by bus, rideshare or a walk of around 45 minutes from the CBD, with the Mt Eden train station nearby. There is free parking at the lower car park on Puhi Huia Road and surrounding streets. The gates are open from 7am until evening, with hours varying by season.
