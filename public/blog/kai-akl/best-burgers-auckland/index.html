Best Burgers in Auckland

Auckland's best burgers run from the smoky Stacked Burger Bar in the west to Big J's super steak burger in the east and the late-night cheeseburger at the White Lady pie cart. The city does both the gourmet build and the honest classic.

Where are the best burgers in Auckland?

Spread across the suburbs, with the west and east holding several of the best. Auckland's burger scene splits between dedicated gourmet burger bars and old-school takeaway joints, and both are worth knowing. The west in particular has a cluster of good ones, but the standout late-night option sits right in the CBD.

The gourmet end

For a built, considered burger, the burger bars deliver. Stacked Burger Bar in the west does a smoked burger with a real wood-smoke profile that sets it apart from fast food. Back to the Kitchen's Baccas2 burger is a bigger, smarter build, and Baby G Burger does a clean, classic cheeseburger. These are sit-down or proper-takeaway burgers rather than a quick fix.

The classics and the institution

Then there is the old-school end. Big J's Takeaways in the east does a super steak burger in the no-nonsense Kiwi takeaway tradition, the kind of burger that predates the gourmet trend. And the White Lady, the historic late-night pie cart in the CBD, does a cheeseburger that is an Auckland institution in its own right, best eaten standing up after dark.

What to expect on price

Burgers here span cheap to mid-range. The takeaway classics like Big J's are easy on the wallet, while the gourmet burger bars sit a bit higher for the bigger, built burgers. None of it is fine-dining money, which is part of the appeal. A burger is Auckland's reliable, good-value meal whatever your budget.
